# Timeline & Audit Trail

We're implementing the Timeline & Audit Trail module for the Legal Case Management Platform.

## Objective

Implement a centralized activity timeline and audit trail system that records significant events occurring throughout the platform.

The Timeline provides a chronological history of activities related to a Case, while the Audit Trail ensures accountability and traceability for legal operations.

This module should automatically record events generated by existing business modules without containing business logic itself.

Reuse the existing Authentication, Authorization (RBAC), User Management, Case Management, and Document Management modules.
