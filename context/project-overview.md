# AI-Powered Legal Case Management Platform

## Overview

The AI-Powered Legal Case Management Platform is a collaborative web application designed to streamline the management of legal cases throughout their entire lifecycle. The platform connects **administrators, assigned lawyers, and court representatives** in a centralized workspace where they can collaborate, track case progress, manage legal documents, exchange updates, and generate reports.

At its core, the platform integrates **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, and **Agentic AI** to provide intelligent assistance for semantic search, document analysis, legal information extraction, report generation, and question answering. Every stakeholder remains synchronized through **real-time updates**, while notifications are delivered through the platform, email, and WhatsApp. The platform also supports **Arabic and French**, ensuring accessibility for all users.

---

## Goals

1. Develop a complete collaborative Legal Case Management Platform connecting administrators, lawyers, and courts through a centralized workspace.

2. Integrate an intelligent AI legal assistant capable of understanding legal documents, answering questions, generating summaries, extracting structured information, and producing legal reports.

3. Ensure real-time synchronization of case updates, multilingual collaboration, and instant notifications through the platform, email, and WhatsApp while maintaining a scalable, secure, and modular architecture.

---

## Core User Flow

1. Administrator signs into the platform.
2. Administrator creates a new legal case.
3. One or more lawyers are assigned to the case.
4. Assigned lawyers receive real-time notifications and gain access to the case.
5. Legal documents are uploaded and automatically processed by the AI pipeline.
6. Court updates, hearings, and decisions are added throughout the case lifecycle.
7. The AI Assistant analyzes documents, answers questions, generates summaries, extracts information, and produces reports.
8. Every update is synchronized instantly between administrators, assigned lawyers, and court representatives.
9. Notifications are delivered through the platform, email, and WhatsApp.
10. Every action is recorded in audit logs until the case is closed.
